Europe Unveils a Record 35 New NVIDIA AI Supercomputers

22.06.2026 nvidianews.nvidia.com

Twenty-three countries are building AI infrastructure for more than 3 million researchers, with 800 AI exaflops deployed or announced in a single year — Europe's largest supercomputing expansion ever.

NVIDIA announced that a record 35 new AI and HPC supercomputers are in development across Europe, spanning 23 countries and set to serve over 3 million researchers. The company calls it Europe's largest single-year supercomputer expansion, with 800 AI exaflops of computing deployed or announced since the previous year.

The headline systems span the continent. The Barcelona Supercomputing Center is upgrading MareNostrum5 with GB300 NVL72 and GB200 NVL4 systems on Quantum-X800 InfiniBand, targeting roughly 20 exaflops of training performance and 33 for inference. Italy's IT4LIA is the giant of the batch: more than 8,000 GPUs delivering 82 exaflops of training and 164 of inference. Bavaria's "Blue Swan" brings 1,000 GPUs to FAU Erlangen and LRZ; HLRS's HammerHAI becomes Germany's first "AI factory" with over 850 GPUs; and Sweden's NAISS Mimer adds 400 more.

The systems are built on NVIDIA's Blackwell and Hopper platforms with Quantum InfiniBand networking, and run the company's full software stack — CUDA-X libraries, NIM microservices and NVIDIA AI Enterprise.

The stated missions read like a map of Europe's scientific priorities: climate modeling, biomedical research, clean-energy development and quantum computing. One notable industrial partnership pairs the infrastructure with Siemens Energy, which is using NVIDIA Omniverse and simulation technologies to design hydrogen-capable gas turbines.

"AI is the new instrument of science, and Europe is building the infrastructure," NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang said of the expansion — a framing the numbers make hard to dispute. After years of worrying about falling behind the US and China on compute, Europe is now assembling sovereign AI capacity at a pace it has never managed before.
