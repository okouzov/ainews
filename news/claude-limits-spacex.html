Anthropic Raises Claude Limits and Signs a SpaceX Compute Deal

06.05.2026 anthropic.com

Claude Code rate limits double across paid plans as Anthropic secures more than 300 megawatts of new capacity — over 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs — through a partnership with SpaceX.

Anthropic announced a double dose of capacity news: meaningfully higher usage limits for Claude, and the compute deal with SpaceX that makes them possible.

Three changes took effect immediately. Claude Code's five-hour rate limits doubled for Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans. The peak-hours limit reduction that previously throttled Pro and Max accounts during busy periods was removed entirely. And rate limits for Claude Opus on the API were increased substantially.

Behind the new headroom sits a new partner: SpaceX. The agreement gives Anthropic access to more than 300 megawatts of new capacity — comprising over 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs — with the capacity coming online within the month. More strikingly, Anthropic said it is interested in developing "multiple gigawatts of orbital AI compute capacity" with SpaceX as a future component of the partnership, hinting at data centers beyond the planet's surface.

The SpaceX deal joins an already remarkable portfolio of compute agreements. Anthropic's announcement points to capacity arrangements with Amazon (up to 5 gigawatts, with nearly 1 gigawatt expected by the end of 2026), Google (5 gigawatts beginning in 2027), a $30 billion Azure capacity commitment involving Microsoft and NVIDIA, and a $50 billion investment with Fluidstack.

The through-line is straightforward: demand for Claude — particularly for agentic coding through Claude Code — has been outrunning supply, and Anthropic is buying power wherever power can be found. For users, the practical effect is fewer interrupted sessions and more room to run long agentic workloads; for the industry, it is one more signal that the frontier-AI race is increasingly decided by megawatts as much as by model weights.
