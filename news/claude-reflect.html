Claude's New "Reflect" Dashboard Shows You Your Own AI Habits

09.07.2026 techcrunch.com

The built-in dashboard visualizes what you discuss with Claude, how often and for what kinds of tasks — with quiet hours and break nudges included. TechCrunch argues it's also quietly selling you on AI.

Anthropic introduced "Reflect," a built-in dashboard that lets Claude users track and visualize their own usage patterns and, more broadly, their AI habits. Reflect launches in beta for Free, Pro and Max users who have the memory feature enabled.

The dashboard shows the topics you explore with Claude, your overall usage patterns and the types of tasks you delegate to the assistant. Sensitive conversations appear only as high-level summaries, and health-related data is excluded altogether. Anthropic says the feature will eventually also show how much time you spend using Claude.

Alongside the analytics, Reflect ships a set of mindfulness controls: "quiet hours" you can schedule, nudge reminders that suggest taking a break from AI work, and reflective prompts — including one that asks which tasks you would rather keep doing yourself, even when AI would be faster.

TechCrunch's Sarah Perez sees a second agenda beneath the wellness framing. While Reflect appears purely informational, she argues, visualizing your reliance on Claude subtly reinforces how indispensable it has become. She draws a parallel to Gmail's 2012 email-analytics experiment: a tool that deepens workflow integration, discourages switching to competitors, and — through recommendations of features like Claude's Projects — actively trains users to use the product more effectively.

Both readings can be true at once. Reflect gives users an unusually honest mirror of their AI dependence — and every glance in that mirror is also a reminder of how much of their working life now runs through Claude.
